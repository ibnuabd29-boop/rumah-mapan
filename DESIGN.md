# Rumah Mapan visual direction

## Design read

Rumah Mapan is a family progress app for Indonesian households, used for short daily activities on phones and larger screens. The visual language is warm and welcoming, with clearer color, stronger hierarchy, and small moments of delight grounded in the idea of building a home together.

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Decisions

- Palette: keep the existing forest green, warm paper, timber, and roof-tile yellow so the app feels settled and home-centered rather than like a generic productivity tool.
- Typography: use Fraunces for headings to echo a home's signboard, and Public Sans for readable forms and family content.
- Layout: use a compact sticky app header and bottom navigation; put today's challenge first, followed by family progress and supporting information.
- Admin progress: show one real-data row per family with approved points, completed challenges, pending evidence, and activity; link each row to its existing family detail rather than inventing a progress percentage.
- Surfaces: use rounded, bordered cards for the daily task, real progress metrics, and feature destinations; keep supporting copy on the page rather than boxing every section.
- Identity motif: reuse the existing line drawing of a house frame to connect the brand with family progress; do not add stock illustrations or substitute logos.
- Icons: use small custom line drawings for navigation, family features, and real progress metrics. Their square-ended strokes echo the house-frame motif, while visible labels keep destinations clear.
- Accent: reserve roof-tile yellow for today's challenge and a few meaningful moments so the main action stands out without turning every card into a highlight.
- Motion: keep transitions brief and functional, with no scroll reveals or looping movement, to preserve a calm pace and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Themes: keep both light and dark themes on the same green and timber palette, with contrast checked separately in each theme.
